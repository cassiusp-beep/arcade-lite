# Voice & Tone

> **Template.** Voice is who we are, which stays constant. Tone is how we adapt to the moment.

## Voice
_Three to four traits, each with "this, not that."_
**Provisional:**
- **Playful, not silly:** a magazine quiz, not a meme page.
- **Confident, not bossy:** a stylist friend who has opinions and lets you decide.
- **Specific, not generic:** "Linen, low light, one good lamp," not "cozy vibes."

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Quiz questions | Teasing, quick | _"Be honest: how many throw pillows is too many?"_ |
| Profile reveal | Celebratory, flattering, specific | _"You're a Sunlit Archivist."_ |
| Generating | Calm, transparent about the wait | _"Mixing your palette… about a minute."_ |
| Price / checkout | Plain and clear, no hype | _"$48 · Made to order · Ships in 5–7 days"_ |
| Errors | Honest, with a next step | _"Arcade is taking longer than usual. Here's one we made earlier."_ |
