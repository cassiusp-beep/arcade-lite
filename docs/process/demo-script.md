# Demo Script

**Length:** ~2:30 · **Device:** iPhone in Safari (mirrored) · **URL:** arcade-lite.vercel.app · **Safety:** every step below uses `?demo=1`, so nothing waits on Arcade or Gemini live

## The story in one line
"Everyone deserves a stylist. Arcade Lite learns your taste from your actual life, designs something only you would own with Arcade, and shows it on you or in your space, in your light, before you buy."

## Beats

| Time | Beat | Tap | Say | If it stalls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Hook** | Landing page | "Generative commerce gives you infinite options and a blank prompt box. Most of us don't know how to describe our taste. We miss the stylist." | n/a |
| 0:15 | **Vibe check** | `/quiz?demo=1`, answer 3 questions fast (Saturday, caffeine, group chat) | "No 'what's your aesthetic.' Real life: your coffee order, your shoes, your role in the group chat. Watch the aura build with every answer." | Skip quiz |
| 0:45 | **Soundtrack** | Skip quiz → artists are prefilled → **Read it** → **See my style** | "Your music shapes your style too." | It's instant in demo mode |
| 1:00 | **Profile reveal (wow #1)** | The card develops | "That's my style, palette and keywords, shareable. Live, Gemini names it, like 'Velvet Nightshade'." | n/a |
| 1:15 | **Design with Arcade** | `/design?demo=1` → Decorative Pillows → Design | "Arcade turns that profile into a real made-to-order product, with a real price." | Cached design appears in ~6s |
| 1:35 | **See it (wow #2)** | See it in my room → compass/tap West → Next → Sun-drenched → Next → Sage → Next → **View in your room** (AR) | "Point your phone at your window. Here's how it ages in *your* light at year 1, 3 and 5, then it's in my room in AR, built from the product's real size and trim." | Skip AR, show the 3D spin |

## Pre-demo checklist
- [ ] Phone charged, Do Not Disturb on, brightness up, Safari tabs closed
- [ ] Open `arcade-lite.vercel.app` once on the venue Wi-Fi to warm it up
- [ ] Open `/tryon?demo=1` once beforehand, so the ~15 MB face model is cached and allowed camera access
- [ ] Allow Motion & Orientation for the compass (or plan to tap West)
- [ ] Never actually pay on stage
