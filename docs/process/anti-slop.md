# Anti-Slop

> **Template.** These are the rules that keep the work from looking or sounding like generic AI output. Add your own and cut what you disagree with. The reviewer and product-designer check against this file.
> The test: *if you swapped the logo, could this be any other AI app?* If yes, it's slop.

## Visual: never
_Add or edit:_
- Purple-to-blue gradients, glassmorphism, or glowing orbs as default decoration
- A grid of identical rounded cards with an icon, a title, and two lines of text
- Centered hero + three feature columns as the default layout
- Emoji as UI icons or headings
- Drop shadows on everything, or every element a different radius
- Stock "sparkles ✨" to signal AI

## Copy: never
_Add or edit:_
- "Unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "effortless", "revolutionize", "Let's dive in"
- Exclamation marks to fake energy
- Filler microcopy ("Something went wrong. Please try again later.") when a specific message is possible
- Talking about the AI instead of the shopper ("Our AI-powered engine…")
- Generic placeholder names and data in the demo

## Interaction: never
- Spinners with no explanation during long waits (say what's happening and roughly how long)
- Modals for things that could be inline
- Dark patterns: fake urgency, hidden costs, pre-checked upsells

## Instead, always
_Add or edit:_
- One clear point of view per screen (editorial layout, a real typographic voice)
- Specific, human copy written for this shopper in this moment
- One memorable interaction, not ten forgettable ones
- Real constraints shown honestly (price, what's generated, delivery)

## Review checklist
- [ ] Could this screen belong to any other app? If so, what makes it ours?
- [ ] Is every word earning its place?
- [ ] Are loading, empty, and error states designed, not defaulted?
